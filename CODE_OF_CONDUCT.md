# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community.
Contributors are expected to:

- communicate with empathy and professionalism;
- focus on the work and give feedback that can be acted on;
- respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints;
- accept responsibility and correct mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
publishing private information, and any conduct that would make participation
unsafe or hostile.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers through the security
contact described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed
promptly and handled as confidentially as possible.
